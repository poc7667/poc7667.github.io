Kio Pass Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Supersedes the policy dated July 25, 2026

In short: Kio Pass is a zero-knowledge password and card vault. Your data is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your master password. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell, or use the contents of your vault, and has no way to read them.

What Kio Pass stores, and where

  • Vault contents — logins, payment cards, notes, membership and store cards, and photos you attach — are encrypted on your device before they are written anywhere.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, the encrypted vault is stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account. The developer has no access to that database and cannot decrypt its contents.
  • Your master password and recovery phrase never leave your device and are never transmitted to anyone.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and Optic ID unlock use the secure hardware and keychain on your device. Kio Pass never receives biometric data.

What the developer does not collect

  • No account registration and no developer-operated server receives your data.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs, and no third-party crash reporting run by the developer.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Camera and photos

When you attach a photo or read a barcode, the camera frames and the photos you choose are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded to the developer. Photos you attach to an item are encrypted together with the rest of your vault.

AutoFill

The Kio Pass AutoFill extension reads your encrypted vault on your device to fill logins in Safari and in apps. It does not transmit any data.

Purchases

Kio Pass Pro is sold through the App Store as a single purchase that covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Apple processes the purchase and may collect purchase and diagnostic information under Apple's privacy policy. The developer receives no payment details.

Your choices

  • You can delete items, or your entire vault, from the app at any time. If iCloud sync is on, deletions are synced to your iCloud as well.
  • You can remove the app's iCloud data in Settings (iPhone/iPad) or System Settings (Mac) → your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • You can manage or cancel a Kio Pass Pro subscription in your Apple Account's Subscriptions settings.

Children

Kio Pass is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them — it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If Kio Pass features or data practices change, this page is updated with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions and support requests: see the Kio Pass Support page.
