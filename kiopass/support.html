Never post passwords, recovery phrases, card numbers, security codes or any other vault content. The developer cannot read your vault and will never ask for its contents.

Common questions

I forgot my master password

Use the recovery phrase you saved when you set up Kio Pass. Without the master password or the recovery phrase the vault cannot be opened — by design, not even by the developer.

My devices are not syncing

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is turned on, and the device has network access. Some managed (work or school) Apple Accounts do not allow iCloud for third-party apps.

I bought Pro on one device but the other still shows the free tier

Kio Pass Pro is one purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac. On the other device, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
